---
name: handoff
description: Record package status at a milestone. Updates OPS/STATUS.md and lists blockers and the next step.
---
At each milestone for your package:
1. Update your line in `OPS/STATUS.md` (`ID | owner | state | note | HH:MM`).
2. If you are blocked, append a line to `OPS/BLOCKERS.md` (never edit old lines).
3. If you changed or need a change to the API, say so in the note and tell Greatness (contract owner).
4. Print a 3-line handoff: what is done, what is blocked (on whom), your next task.
Keep it short. Do not touch other people's directories.
